Kitten Crawler Man

Kitten Crawler Man is a free dungeon crawler and action RPG that runs in your web browser, on desktop and on mobile. It is an unofficial fan game inspired by Matt Dinniman's Dungeon Crawler Carl.

Play as Carl and Donut

You control two crawlers and can switch between them at any moment; the one you are not playing follows and fights alongside you. Carl is a human melee brawler whose abilities are Smush and Protective Shell. Donut is a cat who casts Magic Missile and can summon her pet, Mongo.

The floors

  • Tutorial: a hand-built first room where Mordecai waits in the safe room.
  • Floor 1, The Dungeon: procedurally generated rooms and halls on a collapse countdown, safe rooms with a Bopca shop, the Defend the Goblin Mother quest, and the bosses The Hoarder and The Juicer.
  • Floor 2, The Dungeon, Level 2: the Krakaren Clone, the Ball of Swine in a colosseum arena, the Grotesque Spider, a prison stealth mission and a pipe puzzle in a clogged bathroom.
  • Floor 3, The Overworld: the Over City, with an inn, shops, the Desperado Club casino, a circus and Big Top maze, a murder mystery, the besieged ratkin village of Briar Hollow, and the Doomsday Scenario finale.

Features

  • Three difficulty levels: Kitten, Crawler and Nightmare.
  • Loot, equipment, achievements and loot boxes from Bronze to Celestial.
  • Hireable mercenaries, the Meat Shields.
  • Gathering, crafting and building to defend Briar Hollow from a siege.
  • Casino games: blackjack, Texas hold'em poker, slots and a prize wheel.
  • Saves in your browser at safe rooms; installable as an app and playable offline.

Controls

  • Move: WASD or the arrow keys
  • Attack or interact: Space
  • Switch between Carl and Donut: Tab
  • Drink a potion: Q
  • Hotbar: 1 to 8
  • Inventory: I. Gear: G. Mini-map: M. Quest journal: J. Follower orders: F.
  • Pause: Escape. Every key can be rebound from the pause menu.
  • On phones and tablets, on-screen touch controls replace the keyboard.
