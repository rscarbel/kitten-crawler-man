KITTEN CRAWLER WORLD

Kitten Crawler World

Kitten Crawler World is a free dungeon crawler and action RPG that runs in your web browser, on desktop and on mobile.

Play as yourself and Belle

You control two crawlers and can switch between them at any moment; the one you are not playing follows and fights alongside you. The human crawler is yours: you give them a name and choose their body and complexion when you start a new game. They fight in melee, and their abilities are Seismic Stomp and Energy Shield. Belle is a sleek tortoiseshell cat who loves heat and wants to cuddle with everyone; she casts Magic Missile and can summon her pet Deinonychus.

The floors

  • Tutorial: a hand-built first room where Dave meets you in the safe room.
  • Floor 1, The Dungeon: procedurally generated rooms and halls on a collapse countdown, safe rooms with a Bopca shop, the Defend the Goblin Mother quest, and the bosses The Trash Gremlin and Big Rooster.
  • Floor 2, The Dungeon, Level 2: the Krakaren Clone, the Ball of Swine in a colosseum arena, the Grotesque Spider, a prison stealth mission and a pipe puzzle in a clogged bathroom.
  • Floor 3, The Overworld: Taloncrest, with an inn, shops, the Cloak & Dagger casino, a circus and Starlight Tent maze, a murder mystery, the besieged ratkin village of Briar Hollow, and the Doomsday Scenario finale.

Features

  • Three difficulty levels: Kitten, Crawler and Nightmare.
  • Gear from Common to Divine, with traits, sets, relics and claws, and achievements that pay reward chests.
  • Hireable mercenaries, The Barracks Mercenaries.
  • Gathering, crafting and building to defend Briar Hollow from a siege.
  • Casino games: blackjack, Texas hold'em poker, slots and a prize wheel.
  • Saves in your browser at safe rooms; installable as an app and playable offline.

Controls

  • Move: WASD or the arrow keys
  • Attack or interact: Space
  • Switch between your human crawler and Belle: Tab
  • Drink a potion: Q
  • Hotbar: 1 to 8
  • Inventory: I. Gear: G. Mini-map: M. Quest journal: J. Follower orders: F.
  • Pause: Escape. Every key can be rebound from the pause menu.
  • On phones and tablets, on-screen touch controls replace the keyboard.
